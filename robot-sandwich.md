# How to make a Peanut Butter and Jelly sandwich

## Assumptions
- The robot is standing at a clean kitchen counter with two clean, empty hands.
- There are two slices of bread lying flat on the counter, side by side.
- A clean plate is on the counter in front of the robot.
- A jar of peanut butter and a jar of jelly are on the counter. Both jars have screw-on lids, but their inside safety seals have already been removed.
- A clean butter knife and a napkin are next to the plate.
- The robot can pick up objects, twist jar lids, and use the butter knife to spread food.
- The goal is to make one sandwich using the two slices of bread.

_Steps_
1. Pick up the first slice of bread.
2. Place that slice flat in the center of the plate.
3. Pick up the peanut butter jar with your left hand.
4. Grip the jar lid with your right hand.
5. Turn the lid counterclockwise until it comes off the jar.
6. Put the lid on the counter with its inside facing up.
7. Set the open peanut butter jar on the counter.
8. Pick up the butter knife by its handle with your right hand.
9. Put the rounded end of the knife into the peanut butter.
10. Scoop up about one tablespoon of peanut butter.
11. Move the knife over the slice of bread on the plate.
12. Place the peanut butter in the middle of the bread.
13. Spread the peanut butter across the top of the bread, stopping from edge to edge
14. Wipe both sides of the knife's blade on the napkin until no peanut butter is visible.
15. Put the knife on the napkin.
16. Pick up the jelly jar with your left hand.
17. Grip the jelly jar lid with your right hand.
18. Turn the lid counterclockwise until it comes off the jar.
19. Put the lid on the counter with its inside facing up.
20. Set the open jelly jar on the counter.
21. Pick up the knife by its handle with your right hand.
22. Put the rounded end of the knife into the jelly.
23. Scoop up about one tablespoon of jelly.
24. Place the jelly in the middle of the second slice of bread.
25. Spread the jelly across the top of that slice, stopping from edge to edge
26. Put the knife on the napkin.
27. Pick up the second slice of bread by its edges.
28. Turn that slice over so the jelly faces down.
29. Lower it onto the slice with peanut butter, lining up the edges.
30. Let go of the bread.
31. Put the lid back on the peanut butter jar.
32. Turn the peanut butter lid clockwise until it is snug.
33. Put the lid back on the jelly jar.
34. Turn the jelly lid clockwise until it is snug.

## Done When
The sandwich is finished when the two slices of bread are stacked on the plate with the peanut butter and jelly between them, and both jars are closed.
